Little Timmy SMS

Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the Little Timmy SMS service operated by Tim Friend at +1 256-801-0291, a private, low-volume conversational AI assistant used for message replies and user-requested reminders.

Information collected

The service may process your mobile phone number, message content, message timestamps, delivery information, consent and opt-out status, and limited technical or operational logs required to provide and secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide conversational responses, deliver requested reminders or status notifications, maintain conversation context, prevent abuse, troubleshoot delivery, and comply with applicable legal or carrier requirements.

SMS program disclosures

Little Timmy SMS sends conversational AI replies and only the reminders or status notifications an authorized user specifically requests. Message frequency varies based on user activity and requested reminders. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

Service providers

Mobile numbers and message content may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the service, including telecommunications providers, Twilio, hosting providers, and AI model providers. These providers process information on behalf of the service for operational purposes.

Mobile information, SMS consent, and opt-in data will not be shared, sold, rented, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to operate, secure, and maintain the service or meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Do not submit passwords, authentication codes, financial information, health information, or other sensitive data by SMS.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out does not affect information that must be retained for security, compliance, or suppression-list purposes.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 18 and does not knowingly seek information from them.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or assistance, text HELP to +1 256-801-0291.
