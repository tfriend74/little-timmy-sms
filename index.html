Conversational AI by SMS

Little Timmy SMS

A private, low-volume AI assistant operated by Tim Friend for conversational replies and user-requested reminders.

How the messaging program works

Little Timmy SMS is operated by Tim Friend and communicates from +1 256-801-0291. Access is limited to people expressly authorized by the service operator. The service does not send marketing messages or purchase contact lists.

An authorized user opts in by initiating a text conversation and sending START to +1 256-801-0291. By doing so, the user agrees to receive conversational AI responses and reminders or status notifications the user specifically requests.

  • Message frequency varies according to user activity and requested reminders.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help.

Important limitations

SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, financial information, health information, or other sensitive data. AI-generated responses may be incomplete or incorrect and should not replace professional judgment.
